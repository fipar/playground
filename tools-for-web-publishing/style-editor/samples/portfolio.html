Central Park in Autumn

Published on October 24, 2024 · Photography

A quiet morning walking through the tree-lined paths of Central Park. The golden leaves reflect the early sunlight, creating a warm canopy above the stone bridges.

Behind the Scene

Shot with a 35mm prime lens on Kodak Portra 400. The atmosphere was crisp and still before the city woke up.
